## 🧠 About Me

I'm wrapping up my **Master's in Data Science & Analytics at UMass Amherst** (GPA: 3.90) after a B.Tech in AI & Data Science from Vishwakarma Institute of Technology.

I sit at the crossroads of **research and production** — I've published peer-reviewed computer vision work with Springer and IEEE, run statistical experiments on 30K+ observation datasets, and shipped real ML systems for 10+ B2B clients as co-founder of **Xresilient**.

That startup experience changed how I think about data work: it's not enough to build a great model — it has to solve something real, communicate clearly to stakeholders, and actually get *used*.
